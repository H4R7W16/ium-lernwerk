## Verwendete Prompts (eingebautes Imagegen)
### Schulfest
Use case: scientific-educational. Create one high-quality editorial story illustration for German schoolchildren age 10–11 learning how cropping influences perception. Landscape 1536x1024. Scene: a fictional school festival in a German school courtyard on a bright overcast afternoon. Composition CRITICAL: left half (x 0–50%) shows a completely empty set of wooden audience benches facing a small empty stage at far left. Right half (x 55–100%) shows about 12 diverse children aged 10–11 and two adults busy together at two colourful craft/activity tables beneath modest triangular bunting. Keep the bench area clearly separated from the activity area so a crop of the left 45% contains only empty benches and stage, while a crop of the right 45% shows busy activity. Whole scene is ONE coherent wide view at human eye level, consistent perspective, no panels or dividers. Polished restrained watercolour and coloured-pencil editorial illustration, natural proportions, precise recognisable actions, muted teal, warm amber, school brickwork, green trees. Children working with paper crafts. Do NOT add any writing, labels, numbers, logos, speech bubbles or watermarks. Do not show cameras or interfaces. No children sitting on the audience benches. The illustration will be explicitly labelled as AI-generated fictional classroom material in the surrounding HTML. The pedagogical point is that empty benches do not prove the entire festival was empty. Save generated output as a file.
### Bibliothek
Use case: scientific-educational. Create one high-quality editorial story illustration for German schoolchildren age 10–11 learning how cropping influences perception. Landscape 1536x1024. ONE coherent fictional school library interior. Composition CRITICAL: left half (x 0–48%) contains an EMPTY inviting reading nook: three vacant comfortable chairs, bookshelf and a window. No people in the left half. Right half (x 55–100%) shows six diverse children aged 10–11 sitting around a table reading and discussing open books with one librarian standing nearby. Clearly readable books and engaged quiet reading actions. Thus a left crop shows no readers while a right crop shows a group actively reading. Continuous coherent room, human-eye-level wide view, no divided panels. Polished restrained watercolour and coloured-pencil editorial illustration, natural proportions, muted teal and warm amber, daylight. No writing anywhere, no legible book titles, signs, numbers, logos, speech bubbles or watermarks, no interface. This will be explicitly labelled as an AI-generated fictional educational scene in surrounding HTML. The learning point is that an empty reading nook does not prove that nobody reads in this library. Save generated output as a file.

## Sichtkontrolle
Beide Szenen sind kohärent. Leere und belebte Bereiche lassen sich getrennt zeigen. Personenanzahlen sind keine Aufgabenbelege; die Illustration ist keine Messung. Sämtliche konkreten Aussagen wurden auf sichtbare Merkmale oder explizite Situationsangaben begrenzt.
